# Design review — 2026-09-08

Phase B of [`spec/ui-conformance-plan.md`](../spec/ui-conformance-plan.md): the 108 artboards
in [`canvas/`](canvas/) checked against the functional spec and against each other, and the
fixes made. This note records what changed and why so the audit that follows starts from a
consistent canvas, not one that still has drift in it.

## 1. Coverage

**FR-UI-1..12.** Every requirement has at least one artboard that fully expresses it; the map is
[`design-intent.md`](design-intent.md) §14. Two corrections to that table came out of this pass:
the pre-existing `Detail.dc.html` is the *inferred* case (K7LWH matched by voice to 02:14:07),
not the confirmed one, so D01/D02 were swapped and `Detail-Confirmed.dc.html` drawn; and the
drawer had no row of its own, so N00 `Menu.dc.html` was added.

**P1–P12.** Each interaction principle has a screen where its cost is visibly paid:

| Principle | Where it is paid |
|---|---|
| P1 uncertainty is content | `States`, `Rows`, all four `Detail-*` states, `Detail-Unknown`'s "what was tried" |
| P2 one tap to inspect | `Detail-Why`; "Why this callsign" inline on every detail |
| P3 correction is cheap and propagates | `Detail-Correct-A/B/C`, `Detail-Propagated` (the count, the list, the undo) |
| P4 capture state never in doubt | the live bar on every running screen; `Capture-Status`; `Capture-Notification` |
| P5 live and record differ | `Rows` hearing/resolving variants; `Log-Partial`; `Flow-Capture` |
| P6 radio vocabulary | over, QSO, net, repeater, simplex, squelch, band, tier, pass — throughout; nowhere "message" or "recording" |
| P7 the log is scanned | `Log`, `Rows`, `Grid`'s fixed column widths |
| P8 setup guided, once | `Setup-*` with a verify state per step; `Flow-Setup`; no continue-anyway on the halt |
| P9 nothing deleted quietly | `Log-Rejected`, `Detail-Revisions`, `Settings-Storage` "never deleted", `Feedback` dialog, `Fail-Reconcile` |
| P10 degradation announced | `Feedback` banner, `Fail-Thermal/Backlog/Storage`, `Flow-Degrade`, the degraded notification |
| P11 weaker device knows less | `Settings-Tier`, `Threads-Ungrouped`, `Fail-Model`, `Improve-Done`'s "0 got worse" |
| P12 provisional is improvable | `Improve-*`, `Now-Idle`'s "Can get better", `Sessions`' tier-1 chip, `Flow-Improve` |

**F1–F22.** Every failure artboard shows §12's *response* column, not only the failure: the halt
in F1 refuses to continue; F2 retries with backoff and records the gap; F5 recovers the session
and re-guides; F6 stops audio before text and never capture; F9 marks the frequency stale and
keeps capturing; F16 is loud; F17 re-queues; F18 is retryable and never blocks; F19 reports both
directions and deletes neither; F20 preserves audio and superseded transcripts. F14 and F22 are
drawn as the screens where the property is visible (a session with a DST change; a calibration
notice) rather than as alerts nobody should see.

## 2. Consistency — what was found and fixed

The 100 new boards were drawn from one token set in one sitting, so the check was mechanical:
every `oklch()` and `font-size` across the canvas, counted, against the guide.

**The guide was wrong, not the boards, in two places.** It had dropped seven greys the *original*
artboards use (`0.90`, `0.88`, `0.80`, `0.72`, `0.68`, `0.60`, `0.55`, `0.50` at hue 250) and
had no entry for 14 / 14.5 / 11.5 / 17px, which the boards use hundreds of times for controls,
row titles, sub-lines and figures. The guide now records them ([`design-guide.md`](design-guide.md)
§3, §4) and states the one chroma rule the originals follow: `0.006` at L ≥ 0.72, `0.008` below.

**Genuine strays, normalised — 43 replacements in 28 boards** (`Editorial` and `Timeline` left
alone; they are the exploration sketches, not the direction):

- Chroma typos: `0.66 0.006` → `0.66 0.008` (8 boards, including one instance in the original
  `Log.dc.html`), `0.62 0.006` → `.008` (5), `0.60 0.006` → `.008` (2).
- Off-ramp greens (`0.42 0.04`, `0.48 0.06`, `0.50 0.07`, `0.52 0.06`, `0.55 0.09`, `0.60 0.11`)
  snapped to the five-step ramp. One of these was a single bar in the original `Main.dc.html`.
- Off-ramp ambers (`0.62 0.07`, `0.62 0.09`, `0.70 0.11`, `0.42 0.06`, `0.45 0.05`, `0.56 0.03`,
  `0.24 0.06`) snapped to the amber set, which now has a four-step chart ramp of its own.
- Grey near-duplicates (`0.44 0.04`, `0.45 0.05`, `0.25 0.010`, `0.32 0.010`, `0.30 0.008`,
  `0.48 0.008`) collapsed onto their neighbours.
- Three off-ramp sizes: a 23px digest-item title and a 22px "rejected" title → 19px; an 8px
  flow-board badge → 9.5px.

**Components the guide did not describe** because the original four boards did not need them:
bottom sheet, step indicator, radio/checkbox/toggle, progress, the hearing/resolving marker
variants, the complete badge set (`REVISED`, `CORRECTED`, tier chip), the stale `?`, the
waveform card, lattice slot and prior bar, the figure tile, the notification. §6.9–6.19 now
specify each, with the values the boards use.

**One invented family, kept:** red (`halt/*`, hue 25) for capture actually stopped. The original
canvas is a two-hue system and this adds a third, so the guide states the rule that keeps it
narrow — red means the operator must act because capture has stopped; every degradation is
amber. It appears on exactly four boards (`Setup-Route-Mismatch`, `Setup-Mic-Denied`,
`Fail-Route`, `Fail-Usb`) plus the Stop action.

## 3. Gaps closed during review

- `Setup-Mic-Denied` — referenced in the intent file's S02 interactions, never drawn. Drawn.
- The S01 "What is captured?" sheet reuses `Settings-About`'s offline-promise copy as a sheet;
  no separate board.

## 4. Left open

- The artboards are static and hand-authored; nothing renders them to PNG for pixel comparison
  against the emulator. Validators compare by eye against the `.dc.html` source. A headless
  render step would tighten Phase E but is not on the critical path.
- Font scaling is asserted (§4 of the guide, the `Type` board's demonstration), not drawn per
  screen. V7 in the plan covers it on the emulator.
- `Editorial` and `Timeline` still carry their original off-token values. They are labelled
  as sketches on their own page and are not audit targets.
